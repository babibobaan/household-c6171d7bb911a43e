03 — The month

The monthly statement

The same numbers as a list. Fixed lines are contracts; the card line is the one we control.

The pressure point

SBI Shinsei runs dry

Salary lands here, but the card, the 2805 loan and Brillia's bills all leave from here too. It is short by every month, and holds only today.

The 30 November tax prepayment of ¥529,800 also comes from here. Without a transfer, the account goes negative in November.

The fix

Chiba refills SBI

Chiba is the emergency fund. A standing rule keeps SBI safe: whenever SBI drops below ¥300,000 at month end, move enough from Chiba to bring it back to ¥500,000. The projection below applies this rule.

Monex

Filled once a year

No monthly transfer. On bonus day, half the bonus () goes to Monex NISA. That money is still ours, so it does not count as spending.
