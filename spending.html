      06 — Talk

      Three questions for tonight

      i.

      What makes us feel good?

      Food, health and beauty matter to both of us. Name the spending that truly adds to our life, and protect it.

      ii.

      What slips through?

      Konbini stops, small app charges, late-night taxis, one-click orders. Small each time, around a month together.

      iii.

      How do we check in?

      Ten minutes on Sunday with the Amex app. If we pass ¥150,000 by the 15th, we slow down for the rest of the month.
